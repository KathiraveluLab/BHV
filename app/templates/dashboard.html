About Behavioral Health Vault (BHV)

Behavioral Health Vault (BHV) is intended to support the documentation of recovery journeys for people affected by serious mental illnesses, substance-use disorders, and other social determinants of health. The central goal is to preserve patient voice through image-based that reflect lived experience over time.

BHV is designed to complement clinical records by providing a space for patient-provided photographs, sketches, scanned drawings, and contextual narratives. These records can be shared directly by patients or documented by social workers during interviews to better represent recovery context.

The broader objective is to make behavioral health data collection participatory, accessible, and ethically grounded, while helping care teams and researchers understand how personal narratives, emotional signals, and social context influence recovery and well-being.

BHV also aims to remain practical for community-facing environments by reducing barriers to use, encouraging adoption in clinics and behavioral health programs where technical overhead can otherwise limit impact.
